# Code of Conduct

JEE Prep Hub is a learning community for students, contributors, and educators. We want everyone to be able to study, ask questions, share knowledge, and improve the platform in a respectful and safe environment.

## Where this applies

This Code of Conduct applies to JEE Prep Hub spaces and activities, including the application and its student community, this GitHub repository, issues, pull requests, project discussions, and any event or communication where someone is representing the project. It applies both online and in person.

## Expected behavior

Participants are expected to:

- Treat people with respect and patience, including when opinions or experience differ.
- Keep feedback constructive and focused on ideas, behavior, or code—not personal attacks.
- Welcome questions from learners with different backgrounds, abilities, and levels of experience.
- Respect boundaries, consent, and requests to stop unwanted contact.
- Keep discussion relevant to learning or the project and make room for others to participate.
- Protect privacy: do not publish another person's personal information, private messages, account details, access codes, or credentials without permission.
- Follow project guidelines and moderator instructions.

## Unacceptable behavior

The following behavior is not acceptable:

- Harassment, intimidation, threats, stalking, or repeated unwanted contact.
- Discrimination, slurs, or degrading conduct based on personal characteristics or identity.
- Sexualized language or imagery, or unwelcome sexual attention.
- Doxxing, sharing private or identifying information without consent, or publishing private student data.
- Impersonation, deliberate disruption, targeted humiliation, or abusive content.
- Spam, scams, or repeated off-topic promotion that disrupts study or project spaces.
- Retaliating against someone for raising a concern or participating in a review.
- Encouraging, assisting, or deliberately evading any of the behavior above.

## Moderation and enforcement

Project maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing this Code of Conduct. They may remove or edit content, issue a warning, close or limit discussion, temporarily restrict access, or permanently remove a participant from a project space. The response will reflect the context, impact, severity, and history of the behavior. Serious or ongoing conduct may result in immediate restrictions without a prior warning.

Maintainers will handle reports as privately as reasonably possible, share details only with people needed to review them, and will not tolerate retaliation against good-faith reporters. Moderation decisions may be explained to the people directly involved; sensitive details will not be published unnecessarily.

## Reporting a concern

Please report conduct concerns privately through the [JEE Prep Hub Contact Admin form](https://jeeprephub.co.in/contact). Sign-in is required to use that form. If you cannot access it, use the contact details available on the [project maintainer's GitHub profile](https://github.com/dhirendra-dev).

Include a description of what happened, where and when it occurred, and links or other context that may help the maintainers review it. Share only information needed to understand the concern. Do not post a conduct report in a public issue, pull request, or student discussion. For security vulnerabilities, follow [`SECURITY.md`](SECURITY.md) instead.

## Questions

For questions about this policy, contact the project maintainers through the reporting channel above.
